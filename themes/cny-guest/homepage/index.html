How a night plays

Ask questions. Trust nobody.

  1. Gather

    The host opens a room. Everyone joins from their phone with the 4-digit code or the QR on the TV.

  2. Search the house

    Roll the die and move. Stepping into a room ends your move.

  3. Suggest

    Name a suspect, this room and an object. The next player clockwise who holds one shows you a card, in secret.

  4. Accuse

    Once, on your turn. Right, and you win. Wrong, and you become a ghost who still has to show cards.
