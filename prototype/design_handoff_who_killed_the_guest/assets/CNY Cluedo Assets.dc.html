How it works
Columns are players. Each row is a card; each column is one of the 6 players at the table.
Marks. ✕ = player doesn't have it, ✓ = player has it, ? = you suspect they do.
Auto-deduction. A ✓ anywhere in a row crosses the card off. If every player is ✕, the row is flagged red as a likely answer.
Case strip. The 3 slots at the top fill in once only one card is left in that category. 指控 opens a confirm sheet.
Tap targets are 40×44px or larger for one-handed play.
